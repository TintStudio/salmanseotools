How to Use the Favicon Generator

  1. Click Select Image and pick your logo or icon (a square image gives the best result).
  2. Press Generate Favicons to create 16, 32, 48 and 180 px versions.
  3. Preview each size and download the PNG files you need.
  4. Copy the HTML snippet and paste it into your page's <head> section.

Why Every Site Needs a Favicon

A favicon is the tiny icon browsers show in tabs, bookmarks and search results. Sites without one look unfinished, while a crisp icon builds instant recognition and trust — a small detail with an outsized effect on how professional your site feels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What image should I use as a source?

A square logo or simple icon works best. The tool centre-crops non-square images to a square before resizing, so wide or tall photos may lose their edges.

Which sizes do I actually need?

16×16 is the classic browser tab icon, 32×32 covers retina displays, 48×48 is used by some browsers and shortcuts, and 180×180 is the Apple touch icon for iOS home screens. This tool gives you all four.

Where do I put the downloaded files?

Upload the PNGs to your website's root folder (or an /icons/ folder) and paste the provided snippet into the <head> of your pages, adjusting the href paths to match where you placed them.

Is my logo uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is read locally and drawn onto canvas elements in your browser. It never leaves your device.
