How to Use the XML Sitemap Generator

  1. Paste your page URLs into the box, one URL per line.
  2. Pick a change frequency and priority, and choose whether to stamp today's date as the last-modified date.
  3. Click "Generate Sitemap" — invalid URLs are flagged and skipped automatically.
  4. Download sitemap.xml, upload it to your site's root, and submit it to Google Search Console.

Why Do You Need an XML Sitemap?

An XML sitemap is a machine-readable list of your site's pages that helps search engines discover and crawl them faster. New sites, large sites and sites with pages buried deep in the navigation benefit the most, because crawlers may otherwise take weeks to find every page on their own.

  • Valid XML output — correct declaration and sitemap namespace, ready for Google and Bing.
  • Automatic validation — lines that are not valid URLs are listed and skipped.
  • Changefreq & priority — tell crawlers how often pages change and how important they are.
  • Optional lastmod dates — stamp today's date to help crawlers spot fresh content.
  • Copy or download — take the sitemap as text or as a ready-to-upload XML file.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many URLs can a sitemap hold?

The official sitemap protocol allows 50,000 URLs per file. This free tool caps at 500 URLs per run — if you need more, generate several files and combine them with a sitemap index.

Where do I upload the sitemap?

Save it as sitemap.xml in your site's root (e.g. https://example.com/sitemap.xml), then submit that address in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

Does a sitemap guarantee my pages will rank?

No. A sitemap helps search engines discover your pages, but ranking still depends on content quality, backlinks and technical health.

What does the priority value mean?

Priority is a hint (0.0 to 1.0) about a page's importance relative to other pages on your site. The homepage is often set to 1.0. It is only a suggestion — crawlers decide for themselves.
