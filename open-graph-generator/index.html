How to Use the Open Graph Generator

  1. Enter the title, description and image you want people to see when your link is shared.
  2. Add the page URL, site name and choose the card type (large image works best for articles).
  3. Click Generate Tags, then copy the code into your page's <head>.
  4. Test the result with Facebook's Sharing Debugger or X's Card Validator.

Why Use Open Graph Tags?

When someone shares your link on Facebook, LinkedIn, X or WhatsApp, the platform builds a preview from your Open Graph tags. Without them you get a plain grey box — with them you get your title, description and a striking image, which earns far more clicks.

  • Richer social previews — control exactly what sharers see on every network.
  • Twitter Cards included — matching twitter:card and twitter:site tags generated together.
  • Clean, valid markup — special characters are escaped so the tags never break your page.
  • Copy or download — paste straight into your HTML or save as a snippet file.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between og:type website and article?

Use "website" for homepages and landing pages, and "article" for blog posts and news — it lets platforms show extra metadata like publish date.

Why isn't my image showing when I share the link?

Common causes: a relative image URL (use an absolute https:// URL), an image smaller than 200×200px, or a cached old preview — re-scrape the URL in the platform's debugger.

Where do I paste the generated tags?

Inside the <head> section of your HTML page, alongside your other meta tags.

Are Twitter Card tags still needed?

Yes. X (Twitter) reads its own twitter:card tags, falling back to Open Graph only partially — including both guarantees a good preview everywhere.
