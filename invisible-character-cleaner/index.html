Why Remove Invisible Characters?

Text copied from websites, PDFs, chat apps and AI tools often carries invisible characters — zero-width spaces, joiners and direction marks you cannot see but software can. They break password fields, corrupt code, confuse plagiarism checkers, and sneak into published articles. A quick scan keeps your text truly clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this invisible character remover really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

What are invisible characters?

They are Unicode characters with no visible glyph — such as the zero-width space (U+200B) — that still occupy a position in the text. They often sneak in when copying from websites, PDFs or chat apps.

Why should I remove them?

Hidden characters can break logins and code, corrupt CSV data, trigger false plagiarism matches, and bloat content. Cleaning them ensures your text behaves exactly as it looks.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The scan runs with JavaScript in your own browser, so your text never leaves your device.
