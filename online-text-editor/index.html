How to Use the Online Text Editor

  1. Click into the editor and start typing, or paste text from anywhere.
  2. Watch the live word, character and line counters update as you write.
  3. Press Download .txt to save your work as a plain text file on your device.
  4. Use Copy to move the text into another app, or Clear to start a fresh note.

Why Use This Text Editor?

Sometimes you just need a blank page — no formatting toolbars, no logins, no cloud sync asking for permission. This editor is a lightweight notepad that opens instantly and keeps your words private on your own device.

  • Zero clutter — one big writing area, nothing to learn.
  • Live statistics — words, characters and lines counted as you type.
  • One-click download — save drafts as .txt files whenever you like.
  • Private by design — everything stays in your browser tab.
  • Works offline — once the page loads, no connection is needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this online notepad free?

Yes — completely free with no sign-up, no ads in your way and no limits on how much you write.

Where is my text saved?

Nowhere. The text lives only in your browser tab while the page is open. Download the .txt file if you want to keep it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser.

What format does the download use?

Plain text (.txt) with UTF-8 encoding, which opens in Notepad, TextEdit, VS Code and every other editor.
