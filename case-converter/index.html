Why Convert Text Case?

Formatting headaches are universal: a spreadsheet export arrives in ALL CAPS, a headline needs proper title case, or a document needs sentences that start correctly after edits. Fixing this by hand in a long document is slow and error-prone — a case converter does it perfectly in one click, preserving your line breaks and spacing.

  • Six case styles — upper, lower, title, sentence, alternating and inverse.
  • Smart title case — capitalises the first letter of every word.
  • Proper sentence case — lowercases everything, then capitalises each new sentence.
  • Live counts — character and word totals update as you convert.
  • 100% private — conversion happens on your device; nothing leaves the page.
  • Free forever — no account, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this case converter really free?

Yes. Like every tool here, it is completely free with no sign-up and no limits.

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalises the first letter of every word, which suits headlines. Sentence case capitalises only the first letter of each sentence, like normal prose.

Does it preserve my line breaks?

Yes. Only the casing of letters changes — paragraphs, line breaks and spacing stay exactly as they were.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion is done with JavaScript in your own browser, so your text never leaves your device.
