Why Do Meta Tags Matter?

Meta tags are the hidden instructions that shape how your page appears in search results and when shared on social media. A sharp title and description win more clicks from Google, while Open Graph and Twitter Card tags control the preview image and headline people see on Facebook, X and LinkedIn.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I paste the generated meta tags?

Inside the <head> section of your HTML page, before the closing </head> tag. If you use a CMS, most SEO plugins have a field where you can paste this code.

How long should my meta description be?

Aim for 140–160 characters. Shorter than that wastes space; much longer and Google will cut it off in the results.

Do keywords meta tags still help SEO?

Google ignores the keywords tag for ranking, but other search engines and internal site tools may still read it. It costs nothing to include, so we generate it for you.

What's the difference between Open Graph and Twitter Cards?

Open Graph tags are the standard used by Facebook, LinkedIn and most platforms; Twitter Card tags are Twitter/X's own format. Including both guarantees a good-looking preview everywhere your link is shared.
