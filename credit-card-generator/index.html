How to Use the Test Card Generator

  1. Choose a card type — Visa, Mastercard, American Express or Discover.
  2. Select how many test cards you need (1, 5 or 10).
  3. Click Generate Cards to get numbers with expiry dates and CVVs.
  4. Copy any row and paste it into your sandbox or staging payment form.

Why Developers Need Test Cards

Payment forms validate card numbers before they ever reach a gateway: the format, the brand prefix and the Luhn checksum must all look right. Real card numbers must never touch test environments, so developers use dummy numbers that pass client-side validation without representing any actual account.

  • Luhn-valid numbers — every card passes the standard checksum validation.
  • Correct brand formats — right prefixes and lengths for Visa, Mastercard, Amex and Discover.
  • Full test details — each card comes with a future expiry date and CVV.
  • One-click copy — copy any number straight into your test form.
  • 100% safe — randomly generated locally; no real accounts exist behind them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can these cards be used to buy anything?

No. They are random numbers with no bank account behind them. They will always be declined by real payment processors — they exist only to pass format validation in test environments.

What does "Luhn-valid" mean?

The Luhn algorithm is the checksum formula payment forms use to reject mistyped numbers. Our generator computes a correct check digit, so the numbers pass that validation like real card numbers do.

Is it legal to generate these numbers?

Yes. Generating random numbers that merely follow a public format is completely legal. They are the standard way developers test checkout flows.

Should I use these in production?

Never. Use your payment provider's official test cards (e.g. Stripe's 4242…) for integration testing, and only real customer cards — handled by the provider — in production.
