How to Use the Keyword Density Checker

  1. Paste your article, blog post or page content into the text box.
  2. Choose a phrase length: single words, two-word phrases or three-word phrases, and set the minimum word length.
  3. Click "Analyse" to see the top 30 keywords with their counts and density percentages.
  4. Adjust your content if any keyword looks over-used, then re-check for a balanced result.

Why Check Keyword Density?

Keyword density shows how prominent a term is inside your content. A healthy density tells search engines what the page is about, while an unnaturally high density looks like stuffing and can hurt rankings. Checking before you publish keeps your optimisation deliberate instead of accidental.

  • 1, 2 or 3-word phrases — spot single keywords and longer keyphrases in one pass.
  • Stop-word filtering — skip filler words like "the" and "and" for cleaner results.
  • Density bars — visual bars make the most frequent phrases easy to compare at a glance.
  • Adjustable minimum length — ignore short words that add noise to your analysis.
  • 100% private — analysis happens on your device; nothing leaves the page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good keyword density?

Most SEO guides suggest roughly 1–2% for a primary keyword, but there is no official rule. Natural, reader-friendly writing matters more than hitting an exact number.

What are n-grams?

An n-gram is simply a phrase of n words: a 1-gram is a single word, a 2-gram is a two-word phrase like "seo tools", and a 3-gram is a three-word phrase. Checking longer phrases helps you find the keyphrases your content really targets.

Why are some words excluded from the results?

Common English words such as "the", "and" and "is" add no SEO value, so they are filtered out by default. You can uncheck the filter to include them.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The whole analysis runs with JavaScript in your browser, so your text never leaves your device.
