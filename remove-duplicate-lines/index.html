How to Use the Duplicate Line Remover

  1. Paste your list into the box — one item per line.
  2. Choose options: keep case-sensitivity on for exact matching, or tick A–Z sorting.
  3. Press Remove Duplicates and see the cleaned list with counts.
  4. Copy the unique lines and use them wherever you need.

Why Remove Duplicate Lines?

Duplicate entries sneak into keyword lists, email lists, URL lists and data exports all the time. Cleaning them by hand is slow and error-prone; this tool scans thousands of lines in milliseconds and keeps only the first occurrence of each.

  • Instant deduping — handles long lists without breaking a sweat.
  • Order preserved — the first occurrence of each line keeps its position.
  • Case control — decide whether "Apple" and "apple" are the same.
  • Optional A–Z sort — get a tidy alphabetised list in one step.
  • Private & free — runs on your device, no sign-up needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which duplicate is kept?

The first occurrence. If a line appears three times, the first one stays in its original position and the later two are removed.

What does case-sensitive mean here?

With it on, "Apple" and "apple" are treated as different lines and both are kept. Turn it off and they count as the same line.

Are empty lines removed?

Yes — blank lines are dropped from the result so your list stays clean.

Is there a limit on list size?

No hard limit. The tool comfortably handles tens of thousands of lines in a modern browser.
