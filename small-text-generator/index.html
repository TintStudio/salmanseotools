Frequently Asked Questions

Is this small text generator really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Will the fancy text work on Instagram and WhatsApp?

In most cases, yes. The styles are built from Unicode characters, which nearly all modern apps and phones can display and accept as plain text.

Are these real fonts?

No — they are Unicode symbol characters that look like styled letters. That is exactly why they work without installing anything.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs with JavaScript in your own browser, so your text never leaves your device.
