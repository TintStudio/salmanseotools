Why Use Salman SEO Tools?

Salman SEO Tools is a lightweight collection of free utilities for writers, bloggers, students, developers and SEO professionals. Every tool runs entirely in your browser with plain JavaScript — your content is processed on your own device and never sent to a server.

  • Completely free — all 46 tools, unlimited use, no account required.
  • Private by design — text you paste and files you open stay on your device. A few tools (PDF, OCR, QR) load an open-source JavaScript library from a CDN, but your files are still processed locally and never uploaded.
  • Fast and simple — no downloads, no waiting rooms, results in one click.
  • Mobile friendly — every tool works smoothly on phones and tablets.

Yes — every tool on this site is free to use with no sign-up, no trial period and no hidden limits.

Yes. All tools run client-side in your browser. Nothing you type, paste or upload is sent to any server.

No. The tools work directly in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile.
