How to Use the Image Resizer

  1. Choose an image from your device (JPG, PNG, WebP and most other formats work).
  2. Pick a resize mode: exact width × height, a percentage of the original, or a maximum width.
  3. Tick "Keep aspect ratio" to avoid stretching, choose the output format and quality, then press Resize Image.
  4. Preview the result and download it with one click.

Why Resize Images?

Oversized images are one of the most common reasons pages load slowly — and slow pages rank worse and convert worse. Resizing images to the exact dimensions your layout needs keeps file sizes small and pages snappy, whether it's a blog hero image, a product photo or a social media banner.

  • Three resize modes — exact dimensions, percentage scaling, or max-width fitting.
  • Aspect ratio lock — never stretch or squash an image by accident.
  • Format conversion built in — resize and convert to JPEG, PNG or WebP in one step.
  • Live quality control — balance file size against visual quality with the slider.
  • Fully private — everything happens on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Resizing is done with the canvas API in your own browser, so your images never leave your device.

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Shrinking an image keeps it sharp. Enlarging beyond the original size can look soft, so we recommend resizing down rather than up, and using 80–90% quality for JPEG/WebP.

What is the difference between the resize modes?

Exact dimensions sets a fixed width and height. Percentage scales the original up or down. Max width shrinks wide images to fit a limit while keeping their proportions.
