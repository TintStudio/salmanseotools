How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth from the date picker above.
  2. Click Calculate Age.
  3. See your exact age in years, months and days, plus bonus stats below.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

Forms, applications and eligibility checks often ask for your exact age — not just the year count. Instead of doing calendar math by hand, this calculator handles month lengths and leap years for you and even tells you which weekday you were born on.

  • Exact age — precise years, months and days as of today.
  • Bonus stats — total days lived, total months, birth weekday and days until your next birthday.
  • Leap-year aware — correct results even for February 29 birthdays.
  • 100% private — everything is computed on your device.
  • Free forever — no account, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the exact age calculated?

We subtract your birth date from today's date, borrowing months and days where needed — accounting for different month lengths and leap years.

What if I was born on February 29?

The calculator still works: in non-leap years your "birthday" is treated as March 1 for the next-birthday countdown.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The date is used only for the calculation in your browser and is never sent to any server.

Can I calculate someone else's age?

Yes — just enter their date of birth instead of your own.
