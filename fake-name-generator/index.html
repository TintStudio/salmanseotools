Why Use Fake Data?

Real user data should never appear in test environments, screenshots or design mockups — it is a privacy risk and often breaks data-protection rules. Realistic fake profiles let developers and designers work with lifelike content: user tables, checkout forms, email templates and dashboards all look right without touching anyone's real information.

  • Complete profiles — every row includes a name, email, phone, street and city.
  • Realistic formats — believable email patterns and phone formats, not gibberish.
  • Bulk generation — up to 20 profiles per click for seeding test databases.
  • One-click copy — copy individual rows or grab everything at once.
  • Private & free — generated locally in your browser, no sign-up needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these real people?

No. Names and details are assembled randomly from built-in lists. Any resemblance to an actual person is purely coincidental.

Can I use this data in production?

No — this is test data for development, QA and design mockups only. Production systems must only hold real data collected with proper consent.

Is it safe to share screenshots containing this data?

Yes, that is exactly what it is for. Since every profile is fictional, screenshots and demos carry no privacy risk.

How is the data generated?

First and last names, domains, streets and cities are picked at random from built-in lists and combined into plausible profiles — all inside your browser.
