Speech to Text

Speak into your microphone and watch your words appear as text — free voice typing in six languages.

Home / Text Tools / Speech to Text

This tool uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Audio is processed by your browser and never uploaded to our servers.

How to Use the Speech to Text Tool

  1. Pick your speaking language from the dropdown (English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic or Spanish).
  2. Click Start Listening and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  3. Speak clearly — your words appear in the transcript box in real time.
  4. Click Stop when done, then copy the text or download it as a .txt file.

Why Use Voice Typing?

Most people speak three to four times faster than they type. Voice typing turns that speed into an advantage: dictate articles, notes, emails or captions hands-free, then polish the text with our other tools. It is also a practical accessibility aid for anyone who finds typing difficult.

  • Six languages — including Urdu, Hindi and Arabic, not just English.
  • Live interim results — see words appear as you speak, not only at the end.
  • One-click export — copy the transcript or download it as a plain text file.
  • Private by design — recognition runs through your own browser; nothing is stored.
  • Free forever — no account, no time limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which browsers support voice typing?

The Web Speech API works best in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on desktop and Android. If your browser does not support it, the tool will show a clear notice.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

No. Speech recognition is handled by your browser's built-in engine. We never receive, store or transmit your audio.

How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy is very good for clear speech in a quiet room, in all six supported languages. Background noise, heavy accents or mumbling can reduce accuracy — speaking slowly and clearly helps.

Can I add punctuation by voice?

In most languages you can say words like "period", "comma" or "question mark" and the recognizer will insert the punctuation mark.
