How to Use the Article Rewriter

  1. Paste your article, blog draft or paragraph into the input box above.
  2. Click "Rewrite Article" — the tool swaps eligible words with synonyms from its built-in dictionary.
  3. Check the uniqueness score and read through the result to make sure the meaning still flows naturally.
  4. Copy the result, or hit "Rewrite Again" for a fresh variation of the same text.

Why Rewrite Articles?

Repurposing content is a normal part of publishing: product descriptions get reused across marketplaces, news gets summarised for newsletters, and old posts get refreshed for a new audience. A synonym-based rewriter gives you a different surface wording of the same ideas, which is a fast starting point for a second draft — though you should always review and polish the output by hand.

  • Built-in synonym dictionary — over 100 common English words, each with several alternatives.
  • Uniqueness score — see exactly what percentage of words was changed.
  • Proper nouns protected — names and places are never swapped out.
  • Capitalization preserved — sentence starts keep their capital letters.
  • Randomised wording — every click produces a different variation.
  • 100% private — the whole rewrite happens on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this article rewriter really free?

Yes. Like every tool here, it is completely free with no sign-up and no word limits.

Will the rewritten text pass plagiarism checks?

The tool changes wording, not ideas. A high uniqueness score is a good start, but always read the result, restructure sentences where needed, and run it through our plagiarism checker before publishing.

Does my text get uploaded to a server?

No. The rewriting is done with JavaScript in your own browser, so your text never leaves your device.

Why are some words left unchanged?

Words are only replaced when they exist in the synonym dictionary. Proper nouns, brand names and rare terms are deliberately skipped so your text keeps its meaning.
