How to Use the Text Summarizer

  1. Paste your article, essay, report or any long text into the box above.
  2. Choose how many key sentences you want in the summary (1–10).
  3. Click "Summarize" — the tool scores every sentence and keeps the most important ones in their original order.
  4. Copy the result and use it as a TL;DR, abstract, meta snippet or study notes.

Why Use This Summarizer?

Long content takes time to read. Whether you are skimming research, preparing study notes or writing a quick abstract for an article, an extractive summary gives you the core message in seconds — using the author's own words, so nothing is misrepresented.

  • Extractive and transparent — every summary sentence comes verbatim from your text.
  • Frequency-based scoring — sentences are ranked by how strongly they connect to the text's main topics.
  • Original order preserved — the summary reads naturally from start to finish.
  • Adjustable length — pick anywhere from 1 to 10 key sentences.
  • 100% private — summarization happens on your device; nothing leaves the page.
  • Free forever — no account, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this text summarizer really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

How does the summarizer pick sentences?

It counts how often meaningful words appear across the whole text, scores each sentence by the importance of the words it contains, and keeps the highest-scoring sentences in their original order.

Does it rewrite my text?

No. This is an extractive summarizer, so each sentence in the summary is copied exactly as it appeared in your input.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The summarization runs with JavaScript in your own browser, so your text never leaves your device.
