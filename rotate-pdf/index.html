How to Use the Rotate PDF Tool

  1. Choose the PDF file with pages that need rotating.
  2. Pick an angle — 90°, 180° or 270° clockwise — and choose all pages or a selection.
  3. Press Rotate PDF and the rotation is applied instantly.
  4. Your corrected rotated.pdf downloads automatically.

Why Rotate PDF Pages?

Scanned documents and phone photos often land sideways or upside down in a PDF, making them unreadable and unprofessional. Rotating the affected pages puts everything upright for reading, sharing and printing.

  • Whole file or hand-picked pages — fix one bad page or the entire document.
  • Three angles — 90°, 180° and 270° cover every orientation problem.
  • Content untouched — only the page orientation changes, quality stays identical.
  • Fully private — rotation runs locally in your browser.
  • Free forever — no account, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF rotator really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The rotation is done with JavaScript in your own browser, so your file never leaves your device.

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes. Choose "Selected pages" and enter pages like 2,5-7 — only those pages are rotated, the rest stay as they are.

Does rotating reduce PDF quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page orientation flag, so text and images keep their exact original quality.
