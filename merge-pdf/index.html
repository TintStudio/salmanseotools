This tool loads the open-source pdf-lib library from a CDN; your PDFs are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

How to Use the Merge PDF Tool

  1. Click the file picker and select two or more PDF files from your device.
  2. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to arrange the files in the order you want them merged.
  3. Press Merge PDFs and wait a moment while the files are combined.
  4. Your new merged.pdf downloads automatically — check your downloads folder.

Why Merge PDFs Online?

Scattered PDFs are hard to share and easy to lose. Joining invoices, reports, certificates or scanned pages into one file keeps everything in a single, tidy document that is simpler to email, archive or print.

  • Custom page order — rearrange files before merging with one click.
  • No file-size games — merge as many pages as your device can handle.
  • Private by design — documents are combined locally; nothing is uploaded.
  • No watermarks — the output is a clean PDF, exactly your pages.
  • Free forever — no account, no daily limits, no sign-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF merger really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere?

No. The merge happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so your files never leave your device.

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

There is no fixed limit — select as many as you need. Very large files may take a few extra seconds depending on your device.

Will the merged PDF keep the original quality?

Yes. Pages are copied directly from the source files without re-compression, so text and images keep their original quality.
