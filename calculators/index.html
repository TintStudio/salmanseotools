Tips for Accurate Results

  • Use your full date of birth — day, month and year all matter for an exact years-months-days breakdown.
  • Double-check the year — a single wrong digit in the birth year throws the whole result off, so glance at it before calculating.
  • Know what you need — forms usually want years only, while official documents sometimes ask for the full years-months-days figure.
  • Plan ahead — the age calculator also shows days until your next birthday, which is handy for party planning.
