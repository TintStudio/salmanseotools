Why Cite Your Sources?

Proper citations give credit to the authors you build on, let readers verify your claims, and keep your work safe from plagiarism accusations. Getting the punctuation and order right by hand is tedious — this generator formats it for you in seconds.

  • APA 7th & MLA 9th — the two most widely used academic styles.
  • Websites and books — the source types students cite most.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this citation generator really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Which citation styles are supported?

APA 7th edition and MLA 9th edition, for websites and books — the combinations most students and researchers need.

Should I double-check the result?

Yes. The generator follows standard formatting rules, but unusual sources (multiple authors, missing dates) may need manual tweaks — always review against your institution's guide.

Is my input uploaded to a server?

No. The citation is built with JavaScript in your own browser, so nothing you type leaves your device.
