Tips for Working with PDFs

  • Order matters when merging — use the up/down arrows to arrange your files before combining, so chapters and attachments land in the right sequence.
  • Split before you share — instead of emailing a 200-page report, extract just the pages your recipient needs to keep files small and focused.
  • Fix scans in one pass — choose "all pages" in the Rotate PDF tool when an entire scanned document came out sideways, or pick a page range for mixed files.
  • Check the result — after merging or splitting, quickly scroll through the downloaded file to confirm page order and rotation before sending it on.
  • Keep originals — the tools create new files rather than changing your uploads, so your source PDFs are always safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these PDF tools really free?

Yes. Merge, Split and Rotate PDF are completely free with no sign-up, no watermarks and no usage limits.

Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens in your browser with an open-source JavaScript library, so your documents never leave your device.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no artificial limit — the only constraint is your device's memory, since everything runs locally. Very large files (hundreds of megabytes) may be slow on older phones.

Will merging or rotating change my original files?

No. The tools always generate a brand-new PDF for download; your uploaded files are never modified.
