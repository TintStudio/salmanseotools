Text to Image

Turn any text into a clean PNG image with your own colors and sizing.

Home / Text Tools / Text to Image

The image is drawn on a canvas in your browser — your text is never uploaded anywhere.

How to Use the Text to Image Tool

  1. Type or paste the text you want to turn into an image.
  2. Pick a font size and padding, then choose text and background colors.
  3. Click "Generate Image" to render a live preview.
  4. Click "Download PNG" to save the image to your device.

Why Convert Text to an Image?

Quotes, announcements and social-media graphics often start as plain words. Rendering text as a PNG gives you a shareable graphic in seconds — no design software needed. Use it for quote cards, simple banners, thumbnails or watermarks on your own photos.

  • Instant PNG output — preview and download in one click.
  • Custom styling — four font sizes, three padding levels and any colors you like.
  • Automatic line wrapping — long text wraps neatly to fit the image width.
  • 100% private — the image is drawn on your device; nothing leaves the page.
  • Free forever — no account, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this text to image tool really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

What format is the downloaded image?

PNG — a high-quality format that works everywhere, from social media to documents and websites.

Can I use the images commercially?

Yes. Images you generate from your own text are yours to use however you like.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The image is rendered with the canvas API in your own browser, so your text never leaves your device.
