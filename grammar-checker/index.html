How to Use the Grammar Checker

  1. Paste your text into the box above.
  2. Click "Check Grammar" — the tool runs an online grammar check, or a built-in offline check if the service is unreachable.
  3. Read each issue: the message explains the problem, and the highlighted part shows where it is.
  4. Hit "Apply" on any suggestion to fix that spot, or "Apply All Fixes" to accept everything at once, then re-check.

Why Check Your Grammar?

Sloppy writing quietly costs you trust. A reader who spots repeated mistakes stops believing the content, and search engines have said they use quality signals when ranking pages. A quick grammar pass before you publish takes a minute and makes every paragraph read more professionally.

  • Online grammar engine — the check first tries the free LanguageTool service for real rule-based detection.
  • One-click fixes — apply a suggestion directly, or accept every fix at once.
  • Offline fallback — double spaces, repeated words, missing spaces and commonly confused words are caught locally if the online check fails.
  • Sentence context — each issue shows the surrounding text so you know exactly where it sits.
  • No sign-up — paste, check, fix. Nothing to install or register for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this grammar checker really free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no account and no usage caps.

Does my text get uploaded to a server?

The online check sends your text to the public LanguageTool service for analysis. If you prefer to keep text local, that request only happens when you click "Check Grammar" — and the offline fallback never sends anything.

What happens if the online check is unavailable?

The tool automatically falls back to built-in checks: double spaces, repeated words, missing spaces after punctuation, and commonly confused word pairs like your/you're or there/their/they're.

Will it catch every mistake?

No automated checker is perfect. Use this tool as a first pass, then read your text aloud — your own eyes still catch what software misses.
