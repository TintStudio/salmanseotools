How to Use the Image Compressor

  1. Click Select Image and choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file from your device.
  2. Set the maximum width and pick an output format; drag the quality slider to taste.
  3. Press Compress Image to see the new size, savings and a preview.
  4. Hit Download Compressed Image to save the smaller file.

Why Compress Images?

Heavy images are the number one cause of slow websites — and slow pages rank worse and convert worse. Compressing before upload keeps quality high while cutting load times dramatically, which is exactly what search engines reward.

  • Huge savings — photos often shrink 60–90% with barely visible difference.
  • Resize + compress — cap the width so giant camera files become web-ready.
  • JPEG, WebP & PNG — pick the format that fits your needs.
  • Private by design — files are processed locally, never uploaded.
  • Free & unlimited — compress as many images as you like.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will compression ruin my image quality?

At 80% quality, JPEG and WebP compression is virtually invisible for photos while cutting most of the file size. Drag the slider up if you need pixel-perfect quality, or down for maximum savings.

What does the max width setting do?

If your image is wider than the value you set, it is scaled down proportionally before compression. Smaller images keep their original size — they are never enlarged.

Why doesn't the quality slider affect PNG?

PNG is a lossless format, so quality settings don't apply. Choose JPEG or WebP output when you want aggressive size reduction.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The file is read with the browser's FileReader and compressed on a canvas element locally. Nothing ever leaves your device.
