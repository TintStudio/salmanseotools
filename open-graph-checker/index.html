How to Use the Open Graph Checker

  1. Open the page you want to check and view its source (Ctrl+U in most browsers), then copy the HTML.
  2. Paste it into the box above and press "Preview Link Card".
  3. Compare the Facebook/LinkedIn and X previews — check the image, title and description look right.
  4. Fix any missing-tag warnings, update the page, and re-check.

Why Check Your Open Graph Tags?

When someone shares your link on Facebook, LinkedIn or X, the platform builds a preview card from your Open Graph and Twitter Card tags — not from your visible page. A missing image or a truncated title means a dull, unclicked share. Previewing the card before you publish lets you control exactly what millions of potential visitors see.

  • True-to-life previews — cards styled like the real Facebook and X share layouts.
  • Missing-tag warnings — instantly see which core tags are absent.
  • Full tag table — every og: and twitter: tag listed with its content.
  • Image check — confirm your og:image actually resolves and displays.
  • Private by design — parsing happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Open Graph checker really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Why can't I just enter a URL?

Browsers enforce a security rule (CORS) that stops web pages from downloading other websites directly. Pasting the page's HTML source works around this honestly — and keeps everything private with no server in the middle.

Which tags matter most?

og:title, og:description and og:image drive the Facebook/LinkedIn card; twitter:card, twitter:title, twitter:description and twitter:image drive the X card. Get those right and your shares will look sharp everywhere.

The preview image doesn't load — why?

The og:image URL may be relative, broken, or blocking hotlinking. Use a full https:// URL to an image at least 1200×630 px for best results.
