How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type directly into the box or paste any text from a document, article or web page.
  2. Watch the counters update live as you type — no button clicks needed.
  3. Use the stats to hit content targets, e.g. a 1,500-word article or a 160-character meta description.
  4. Copy the text back out or clear the box to start over.

Why Count Words?

Writers, students, bloggers and SEO professionals all work against length targets. Search engines tend to favour in-depth content, clients pay per word, and platforms like meta descriptions or ad headlines enforce strict character limits. A fast, accurate counter removes the guesswork so you can focus on the writing itself.

  • Live counting — every stat updates the moment you type.
  • Reading & speaking time — estimated at 200 wpm reading and 150 wpm speaking pace.
  • Sentence & paragraph breakdown — spot walls of text before you publish.
  • 100% private — counting happens on your device; nothing leaves the page.
  • Free forever — no account, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Does my text get uploaded to a server?

No. The counting is done with JavaScript in your own browser, so your text never leaves your device.

How is reading time calculated?

We divide the word count by 200 words per minute, the average adult reading speed. Speaking time uses 150 words per minute.

What counts as a word?

Any sequence of characters separated by whitespace. Hyphenated compounds like "well-known" count as one word.
