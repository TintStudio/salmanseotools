Tips for Getting the Most Out of Text Tools

  • Chain tools together: draft in the Online Text Editor, check length with the Word Counter, polish with the Grammar Checker, then verify originality with the Plagiarism Checker.
  • Set length targets first: know your word or character limit before you start, and keep the Word Counter open while you write.
  • Rewrite, then review: the Article Rewriter gives you a fresh draft fast — always read the result and adjust tone before publishing.
  • Clean pasted text: text copied from PDFs or websites often hides invisible characters; run it through the Invisible Character Cleaner before analysis.
  • Save your work: most tools offer Copy and Download buttons — grab your result before closing the tab.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these text tools free to use?

Yes. All 16 text tools are completely free with no sign-up, no trial period and no usage limits.

Is my text uploaded anywhere when I use these tools?

No. Every tool runs in your browser with JavaScript, so your text never leaves your device.

Which tool should I use to check an article before publishing?

A good routine is: Word Counter for length, Grammar Checker for mistakes, Plagiarism Checker for originality, and Keyword Density Checker from our SEO tools for on-page balance.

Do the tools work on mobile phones?

Yes. All text tools are responsive and work in any modern mobile browser, including voice typing and OCR.
