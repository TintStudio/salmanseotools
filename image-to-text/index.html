How to Use the Image to Text Tool

  1. Click the file button and choose a photo, screenshot or scanned document.
  2. Select the language of the text inside the image.
  3. Press Extract Text and wait a few seconds while the OCR runs.
  4. Copy the recognized text and paste it wherever you need it.

Why Extract Text from Images?

Text trapped inside images cannot be searched, copied or edited — until you run it through OCR (optical character recognition). Students digitize handwritten notes, businesses pull data from receipts and invoices, and content teams rescue text from old screenshots. Doing it in the browser means no uploads, no waiting rooms and no accounts.

  • Five languages — English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and Spanish recognition.
  • Live progress bar — see exactly how far the recognition has got.
  • Private processing — the image is analyzed on your device; it is never uploaded.
  • Works with photos & screenshots — signs, documents, receipts, memes, anything.
  • Free forever — no sign-up, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the image uploaded to a server?

No. The OCR engine runs entirely in your browser via the Tesseract.js library. Your image never leaves your device.

How accurate is the text extraction?

Accuracy is best with clear, high-contrast text — screenshots and clean scans work very well. Blurry photos, decorative fonts or handwriting may produce errors you will want to proofread.

Can it read handwriting?

It is designed for printed and typed text. Neat handwriting sometimes works, but results vary widely — printed text is far more reliable.

Which image formats are supported?

Anything your browser can display: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP. Very large images are fine, though smaller files process faster.
