Tips for Better On-Page SEO

  • Keep titles and descriptions in range: aim for titles around 30–60 characters and meta descriptions around 120–160 — the Meta Tags Analyzer flags anything outside these ranges.
  • Don't stuff keywords: use the Keyword Density Checker to keep your main terms natural, typically around 1–2% of the text.
  • Audit before publishing: run the On-Page SEO Checker on every important page to catch missing alt text, duplicate H1s or absent canonical tags.
  • Control your social previews: generate Open Graph tags, then preview them with the Open Graph Checker so shares look right on Facebook, X and LinkedIn.
  • Help crawlers find everything: keep a valid robots.txt and an up-to-date XML sitemap, and submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
