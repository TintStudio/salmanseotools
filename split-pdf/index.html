How to Use the Split PDF Tool

  1. Choose the PDF file you want to split — its page count appears instantly.
  2. Type the pages to extract, e.g. 1-3,5,8-10 for pages 1 to 3, page 5, and pages 8 to 10.
  3. Press Split PDF; invalid entries are skipped and listed for you.
  4. Your new split.pdf downloads automatically with just those pages.

Why Split a PDF?

Long PDFs often contain only a few pages you actually need. Extracting them saves you from emailing a 200-page manual when someone asked for one chapter, and keeps sensitive pages out of shared files.

  • Flexible selection — mix single pages and ranges in one go.
  • Instant validation — out-of-range numbers are flagged, never silently dropped.
  • Original quality kept — pages are copied directly, nothing is re-rendered.
  • Fully private — the whole split runs on your device.
  • Free forever — no account, no limits, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF splitter really free?

Yes. Every tool on Salman SEO Tools is free with no sign-up and no usage limits.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Splitting is done with JavaScript in your own browser, so your file never leaves your device.

What happens if I type a page number that does not exist?

It is skipped and shown in a warning message, so you always know exactly which entries were ignored.

Does the original PDF get changed?

No. The tool only reads your file and creates a new PDF with the extracted pages; the original stays untouched.
